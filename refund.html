Refund Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

Registration Confirmation

A seat in a training program is confirmed only after full payment is received.

Participant Cancellation

  • 7 days before training: Full refund
  • 3–6 days before training: 50% refund
  • Less than 72 hours before training: No refund

Participants may transfer their seat to another person from the same organization with prior notice.

Training Rescheduling

If NormSecure reschedules a session, participants may transfer to the next batch or request a full refund.

Training Cancellation by NormSecure

If a session is cancelled by NormSecure, a full refund will be issued.

Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days via the original payment method where possible.
