Refund Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

Registration Confirmation

A seat in a training program is confirmed only after full payment is received.

All training registrations are non-refundable once payment is completed. This is because training seats are limited and preparation begins immediately after registration.

Seat Transfer

Participants may transfer their seat to another person from the same organization with at least 2 days prior notice.

Once the training session has commenced, participant transfers are not permitted. Failure to attend the training session will be treated as a no-show, and no refund, transfer, or rescheduling will be provided.

Training Rescheduling

If NormSecure reschedules a session, participants may transfer to the next batch or request a full refund.

Training Cancellation by NormSecure

If a session is cancelled by NormSecure, a full refund will be issued.

Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days via the original payment method where possible.
