Refund Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

Registration Confirmation

A seat in a training program is confirmed only after full payment is received. All payments are treated as final unless otherwise stated under this policy.

Cancellation & Refund Policy (Training Programs Only)

NormSecure follows a no-cancellation and no-refund policy by default. Limited exceptions apply only to instructor-led training programs, as outlined below.

  • 7 days or more before training: Full refund
  • 3–6 days before training: 50% refund
  • Less than 72 hours before training: No refund

Seat Transfer Policy

Participants may transfer their confirmed training seat to another individual from the same organisation by sharing the replacement participant’s details.

  • Transfer request must be made at least 2 days (48 hours) before training
  • Seat transfer allowed once per registration
  • No additional charges apply for a valid transfer

Training Rescheduling by NormSecure

If NormSecure reschedules a training session, participants may transfer to the next available batch or request a full refund.

Training Cancellation by NormSecure

If a training session is cancelled by NormSecure, a full refund will be issued.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days via the original payment method, subject to payment gateway timelines.
